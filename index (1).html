How to use this
  • Pick a shape, or draw your own by clicking points on the grid.
  • Choose a transformation tab and set its values — the image (result) updates live.
  • On the Translate tab, you can also drag the shape directly.
  • Press Pick centre on grid then click the grid to set a centre for rotation or enlargement.
  • Press Play to animate the transformation.
  • Try Quiz mode to test identifying transformations.
